First Amendment Deep Dive Analysis

Case Petitioner Position & Reasoning Respondent Position & Reasoning Supreme Court Holding
WV State Board of Education v. Barnette (1943) School Board argued compulsory flag salutes build national unity and patriotism during wartime. Barnette family argued forced salutes violate religious prohibitions on idolatry and compel speech against conscience. Public schools cannot compel students to salute the flag or recite the Pledge of Allegiance. Forced speech violates the 1st Amendment.
Boy Scouts of America v. Dale (2000) Boy Scouts argued private organizations have a 1st Amendment right of expressive association to set membership standards. James Dale argued state public accommodation anti-discrimination laws should prevent firing leaders based on sexual orientation. Private groups have a 1st Amendment right to exclude individuals whose presence affects the group's ability to advocate its viewpoints.
